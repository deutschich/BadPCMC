Why Choose BadPCMC?

Created with personal experience to solve a real problem

BadPCMC was born out of necessity. I created this modpack for my own laptop, which couldn't even run Vanilla Minecraft properly. Through careful selection and configuration of performance mods, I transformed my struggling laptop into a capable Minecraft machine.

This modpack is pre-configured for weak hardware, so you don't need to spend hours tweaking settings. Just install and play! Every mod has been selected to balance performance improvements with visual quality, ensuring you still get an enjoyable Minecraft experience.

If your PC struggles with Minecraft, BadPCMC is your solution to finally enjoy a smooth, lag-free experience. Created with love and personal experience—this modpack made Minecraft playable on my underpowered laptop, and it can do the same for you!
